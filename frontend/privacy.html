Privacy

What StrollCall keeps, for how long, and how to clear it.

Where you start

  • Your start point is used to plan the walk. It is kept in a private record that no page shows and that is deleted when the invite expires, 24 hours after it was created. Your route from it is deleted then too; the meeting point, destination and walking times stay with the invite.
  • Your friend never sees your start: their page shows your route only from about 200 m after it, and without your turn-by-turn steps or the places you pass near it.
  • Start points are never written to logs.

While you walk

  • Your live position stays on your phone. The track is never sent or stored.
  • If you leave the route, your current position is sent once to find a new way from there. It is not stored or logged.
  • When you finish, this phone keeps the minutes walked and the names of places you passed, for My walks.

What else is stored

What you type or saySent to Meta's Muse models to understand your wish. Typed text is kept in the invite's private record and deleted at expiry. Voice recordings are not stored by StrollCall.
Shared recapsOnly when you tap Share recap: the meeting point, destination, the walk between them, minutes walked and places passed, never a start point or a place close to one. Deleted after 30 days.
After-walk surveyOnly if you tick the consent box: your three answers and, if you give it, your walk time. No names, no locations. Used for the hackathon report.
Invites after expiryThe invite line, the wishes as understood, the meeting point, destination, shared route and walking times stay on the server, without start points or routes from them.

The app runs on Render (US) with a Neon database (US East). Planning and routing run on our own server. Your browser also loads map images from OpenStreetMap's tile servers (they see which area the map shows), fonts from Google Fonts and scripts from cdnjs and jsDelivr. If a walk ever falls outside our own map, place lookups can go to OpenStreetMap's public Overpass service with the midpoint between you; the app asks for start points inside the covered area to avoid that.

This phone

This browser keeps your invite links and their private keys, your walk history (minutes and place names) and two settings (voice prompts, with a friend or on your own). Nothing else.

This clears StrollCall's storage in this browser. It doesn't change anything on the server: invites expire on their own within 24 hours, and shared recaps after 30 days.
